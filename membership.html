Membership

In order to perform in our concerts, a Student Membership Fee of $75.00 is required for each season, to be paid by e-Transfer at least two weeks prior to the student's first performance. There is an additional $30.00 fee for each additional performing family member. Also, students wishing to perform on a second instrument may do so with the addition of a $30.00 Second Instrument fee.

Payment Details

  • Step 1: Send an e-Transfer for the membership fee(s) to kanatamusicclub@gmail.com. In the message line of the e-Transfer include the student(s) proper performing name as well the Teacher's name. It is important that the student's chosen performing name is consistently used in this payment memo as well as on the Concert Request Form. Please clearly indicate the specifics of any additional fees and for which student it pertains.
  • Step 2: Forward a copy of the e-Transfer to the student's teacher.

Concert Etiquette

  • All performers should arrive at least 5-10 minutes before the concert time, but not earlier than 15 minutes before concert time, to ensure a teacher supervisor is in house.
  • Members must plan to stay for the entire concert that they are performing in, showing support and interest in their fellow club members.
  • No rehearsals are allowed before the concert begins. String and woodwind players may use the piano for tuning purposes before the concert begins.
  • Members should dress appropriately for public performance. No jeans, hoodies or T-shirts. No winter boots on stage.
  • In the case when someone must enter or exit the hall during a concert, please do so very discreetly between performances.
  • You may record your own student/child's performance. Do not record any other performers without their parent's consent. Recording equipment should be set up discreetly before the start of the concert.
  • No food is permitted in the venue. Water is permitted.
